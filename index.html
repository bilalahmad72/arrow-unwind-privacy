Arrow Unwind - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Arrow Unwind ("the app") is a puzzle game published by Apposoft ("we"). This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information we collect

We do not require an account and we do not collect your name, email address, or location. Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, settings) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and general device and app-usage information to serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app asks for your consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice later in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). See Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Third-party services

The only third-party service that receives data is Google AdMob / Google Play services. We do not sell your personal information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and ads are limited to a PG content rating. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. We hold no server-side copy.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
