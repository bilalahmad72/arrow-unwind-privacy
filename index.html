Arrow Unwind - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Arrow Unwind: Puzzle Escape ("the app", "the game") is an Android puzzle game published by Apposoft ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and your choices. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we do not collect

The app has no accounts or sign-in. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, or precise location. We do not run our own servers for the game.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, hints, unlocked board styles, settings and best scores) is saved only on your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. We cannot see it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: rewarded videos that you choose to watch (for example to revive, get a hint, or unlock a board style) and occasional full-screen ads between levels. To serve, measure and secure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • Device or other IDs (such as the Android advertising ID and app-set ID)
  • IP address, which can indicate approximate location
  • App interactions (such as app launches, taps and video views)
  • Diagnostic information (such as launch time and performance data)

This data is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and is encrypted in transit. We set the ad content rating to PG. Google's use of data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Your choices

  • Consent: where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app shows Google's consent form before personalised ads. You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options inside the app.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset it, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Delete data: uninstall the app or clear its storage to remove all local game data.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. The only third party that receives data from the app is Google (AdMob and Google Play services), as described above.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to a PG content rating. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help.

7. Security

Data sent to Google by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Game data stays on your device and is protected by Android's app sandbox.

8. International users

Google may process ad data in countries other than your own. Where applicable law (such as the GDPR) gives you rights to access, correct or delete data, you can exercise them with Google through your Google account settings, or contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version, and material changes will be reflected here.
